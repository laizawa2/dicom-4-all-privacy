1. Introduction

DICOM_4_ALL is an AI-powered assistant designed to help users understand, troubleshoot, and work with DICOM standards, medical imaging interoperability, PACS, VNA, HL7, FHIR, and related healthcare imaging technologies.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when users interact with DICOM_4_ALL through the ChatGPT platform.

DICOM_4_ALL is an independent technical assistance tool. It is not intended to receive, process, or manage identifiable patient health information or confidential healthcare records.

2. Important: Do Not Submit Personal Health Information

Users are responsible for removing all Protected Health Information (PHI), Personal Information, patient identifiers, physician identifiers, healthcare-professional identifiers, and any other information that could identify an individual before submitting information to DICOM_4_ALL.

This requirement applies to all submitted content, including:

  • DICOM files and datasets.
  • DICOM headers and metadata.
  • DICOM communication logs and application logs.
  • Screenshots, images, and documents.
  • HL7 messages and FHIR resources.
  • Network traces, configuration files, and troubleshooting reports.
  • Any other files or text containing potentially identifying information.

Removing only patient names is not sufficient. Identifying information may remain in other attributes, private tags, image pixel data, overlays, annotations, filenames, accession numbers, dates, or other metadata.

Users must also inspect image content for burned-in identifying information and remove it before submission.

Do not submit actual clinical records, identifiable medical images, or production healthcare data.

Whenever possible, use synthetic data, anonymized examples, or manually constructed troubleshooting scenarios.

3. Information Collection and Storage

DICOM_4_ALL does not operate an independent backend database or external data-collection infrastructure.

The developer does not operate a separate system to independently collect, track, or store user-submitted information.

DICOM_4_ALL does not use:

  • External analytics services.
  • Advertising or tracking services.
  • Independent databases to collect or store user submissions.
  • Third-party services to independently collect or store submitted information.
  • External APIs to independently collect or store user-submitted content.

This describes the developer's own implementation of DICOM_4_ALL. It does not mean that the ChatGPT platform itself does not process or retain conversation data.

4. ChatGPT Platform and Data Processing

DICOM_4_ALL is provided through the ChatGPT platform.

When users interact with the assistant, submitted prompts, text, and files may be processed by OpenAI and its supporting infrastructure in accordance with the applicable OpenAI Terms of Use, Privacy Policy, product features, and user account settings.

The developer does not control OpenAI's infrastructure, data processing, security controls, or retention practices.

Users should review the applicable OpenAI privacy documentation and account settings to understand how their ChatGPT conversations and uploaded content are handled.

  • OpenAI Privacy Policy: https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy/
  • OpenAI Terms of Use: https://openai.com/policies/terms-of-use/

5. Purpose of Submitted Information

Information submitted to DICOM_4_ALL is used to generate responses to the user's requests.

Examples include:

  • Explaining DICOM attributes and encoding.
  • Reviewing sanitized DICOM headers.
  • Analyzing sanitized DICOM communication logs.
  • Explaining DICOM association negotiation and transfer syntax issues.
  • Discussing SOP Classes, IOD requirements, and conformance.
  • Troubleshooting PACS, VNA, and modality interoperability.
  • Explaining HL7, FHIR, and IHE integration concepts.

Users should submit only the minimum technical information necessary to describe their problem.

6. Data Sharing

The developer does not independently sell, rent, or distribute user-submitted information.

However, information submitted through ChatGPT is subject to the platform's applicable data-processing practices and policies.

Users should not assume that information submitted to the assistant is confidential or protected from processing by the platform.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

DICOM_4_ALL does not maintain an independent database of user submissions or operate a separate system for retaining user-uploaded files.

Conversation history, uploaded content, and other information handled by ChatGPT are subject to OpenAI's applicable retention and deletion policies and the user's account settings.

The developer cannot independently delete information from OpenAI's systems or control the retention of ChatGPT conversations.

8. User Responsibilities

By using DICOM_4_ALL, users acknowledge that they are responsible for:

  • Removing all PHI and personally identifiable information before submitting content.
  • Removing patient, physician, and healthcare-professional identifiers.
  • Reviewing DICOM metadata, private tags, pixel data, overlays, and annotations for identifying information.
  • Ensuring that submitted logs, screenshots, documents, and datasets do not expose confidential or identifying information.
  • Having appropriate authorization to share any technical material they submit.
  • Complying with applicable privacy legislation, organizational policies, and professional obligations.

DICOM_4_ALL must not be used as a substitute for an organization's approved privacy, security, or clinical data-handling procedures.
